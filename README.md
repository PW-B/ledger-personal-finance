# Ledger

A personal finance app for iPhone and Mac, built for my own household's use.

- Tracks income and expenses across my own bank and credit card accounts
- Imports transactions from bank files and, via Plaid, directly from my banks
- Categorizes spending, flags transfers between my accounts and duplicates
- Tracks family medical claims and insurance/FSA reimbursements

This is a single-user app: it is not distributed, and no data is shared with
anyone else. Financial data stays on my own devices and my personal iCloud Drive.
